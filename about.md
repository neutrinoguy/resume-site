# About Aagam Shah (neutrinoguy)

## Professional Identity & Role
Aagam Shah (professionally known in the security research community as **neutrinoguy**) is a Cyber Security Technical Lead at **ABB** based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. With deep domain expertise in Industrial Control Systems (ICS/SCADA), firmware vulnerability assessment and penetration testing (VAPT), and operational technology (OT) protocols, Aagam leads security architecture, threat modeling, and product security evaluations across mission-critical infrastructure.

In addition to traditional industrial control systems security, Aagam is actively engaged in both **Offensive and Defensive AI Security**. This includes red teaming large language model (LLM) agents, evaluating adversarial prompt injection vulnerabilities, analyzing agent tool-use execution paths, and engineering robust, deterministic guardrails for autonomous multi-agent pipelines deployed in industrial and enterprise environments.
