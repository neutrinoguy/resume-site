POLICY // ZERO-TRACKING ARCHITECTURE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Immediate · Last Updated: October 2026

1. Zero-Tracking & Privacy-First Foundation

This website (https://neutrinoguy.zip), maintained by Aagam Shah (neutrinoguy), is built from the ground up with a strict privacy-first architecture. As a security professional specializing in threat modeling and operational integrity, I adhere to the principle of data minimization:

  • No Commercial Tracking Scripts: This site contains no advertising trackers, behavioral profiling pixels, or cross-domain identifier synchronization scripts.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, or third-party session recording scripts are loaded.
  • Zero Client Cookies: This website does not set tracking, preference, or authentication cookies on your browser.

2. Edge Infrastructure & Transient Logging

This website is deployed across Vercel's global edge network. When your browser or automated client issues an HTTP/HTTPS request to our domain:

  • Basic network connection telemetry (IP address, requested URI path, User-Agent header, and HTTP status code) is processed in transient volatile memory at edge points of presence (PoPs).
  • This telemetry is utilized strictly for routing, performance optimization, rate limiting, and mitigating distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks.
  • Transient edge logs are automatically purged in accordance with standard infrastructure lifecycle schedules and are never converted into user behavioral profiles.

3. Direct Communications & Disclosure Inquiries

When you communicate directly via email (hello@hexlab.xyz) or reach out across developer platforms:

  • The contact details and technical vulnerability data you provide are used strictly to evaluate disclosures, discuss professional engagements, or resolve security findings.
  • Your communications are treated confidentially and are never rented, sold, or shared with commercial entities.

4. Autonomous AI Agents & Content Negotiation

Autonomous AI agents, coding assistants, and search indexing spiders are formally recognized and supported:

  • Machine-readable files (llms.txt, llms-full.txt, agent-instructions.md, and Resume.md) are published openly for factual indexing.
  • Content negotiation via the HTTP Accept: text/markdown header delivers raw markdown representations without tracking or honeypots.
  • Crawlers are expected to adhere to instructions outlined in robots.txt.
